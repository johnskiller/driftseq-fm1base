# Who owns a channel's SOUND. A program change is per channel, not per track:
# the receiver keeps one program per channel, so a channel shared by several
# tracks needs exactly one track to speak for it - the lowest-numbered one WITH
# NOTES. A fresh demo, because this is a property of the pattern and the other
# page tests deliberately leave the pattern edited.
wait 600
tap ENV
wait 20
expect mode 4

# the drum channel: tracks 0,1,2 share ch9 and all three have notes -> track 0
turn SELECT -30
wait 10
expect track 0
expect ts_ch 9
expect ts_owner 0
expect ts_shared 3

# the bass: alone on ch0 in sound (eleven empty tracks also sit there) - the eleven empty tracks 5..15 also sit on ch0 by
# default, and none of them may speak for it (that was the demo's broken sound)
turn SELECT 3
wait 10
expect track 3
expect ts_owner 3
expect ts_shared 12
expect ts_prog 38

# the lead: alone on its channel
turn SELECT 1
wait 10
expect track 4
expect ts_owner 4
expect ts_shared 1

# an empty track on the bass's channel is explicitly NOT the owner, and turning
# its PROG must not disturb the owner's (the page has nothing to do about it
# yet, but the engine must ignore it - asserted on the wire in trkset_route)
turn SELECT 1
wait 10
expect track 5
expect ts_owner 3
turn K1 1
wait 5
turn K2 3
wait 5
expect ts_prog 3
expect ts_owner 3

# ...while the SAME edit on the owner does move the channel
turn SELECT -30
wait 10
turn SELECT 3
wait 10
expect track 3
expect ts_owner 3
expect ts_row 0
turn K1 1
wait 5
expect ts_row 1
turn K2 1
wait 5
expect ts_prog 39
